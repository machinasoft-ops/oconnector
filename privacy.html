O Connector — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 November 2026

O Connector is an iPhone application that lets you open Odoo servers you already have access to. This policy explains what the app does with your data. The short version: the app has no servers of its own, collects no analytics, and sends data only to the Odoo server you chose.

1. Who is responsible

The application is published by Nikita Sedykh PR Beograd (registered entrepreneur, Belgrade, Serbia) (“we”). Contact: hi@sftmchn.tech.

Each Odoo server you add to the app is operated by its own owner (your employer, your company or whoever gave you the address and login). That owner is responsible for the data stored on that server and for its own privacy policy. We do not have access to servers we do not operate.

2. What the app stores on your phone

  • The list of servers you added: name and address.
  • Sign-in sessions (cookies) for those servers, so you do not have to log in every time.
  • Files you chose to download or open.
  • The push notification token issued by Apple and the unread counters received from your servers.

All of this stays on your device. Removing a server from the list deletes its session; deleting the app deletes everything.

3. What is sent to your Odoo server

The app shows your server's web interface. Everything you type, send or upload goes directly to that server over HTTPS, exactly as it would from a web browser. In addition, when the server has the companion notification module installed, the app sends it:

  • the Apple push token of this phone, so the server can deliver notifications;
  • a device name and the app version, so you can recognise and remove the phone in your server settings.

The server can revoke the token at any time, and the app removes it when you sign out or delete the server.

4. Push notifications

Notifications are delivered through the Apple Push Notification service. Their content (for example the sender's name and the text of a message) is prepared by your Odoo server; what is included depends on the server's settings. Apple's handling of push traffic is described in Apple's own privacy policy.

5. Camera, microphone and photos

The app asks for camera, microphone and photo library access only when a page on your server needs it — for a call, a voice message or an attachment. The media goes to that server and nowhere else. You can withdraw these permissions in iOS Settings at any time.

6. What we do not do

  • We do not collect analytics, crash reports or usage statistics.
  • We do not use advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not sell, share or transfer your data to third parties.
  • We do not run any server that your data passes through.

7. Children

The app is a business tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.

9. Contact
