Your servers, in your pocket

O Connector is an iPhone client for Odoo servers. Enter the address you use in a browser, sign in, and work — with push notifications and native camera and files.

Coming soon to the App Store

Works with your own server

No preset servers and no accounts with us. The app talks directly to the Odoo server you add — Community or Enterprise, version 16 and up.

Several servers, one app

Head office, a subsidiary, a test instance: keep them all in one list, stay signed in to each, switch with a swipe.

Notifications that find you

New messages arrive as push notifications, the badge counts unread across servers, and a tap opens the right chat on the right server.
